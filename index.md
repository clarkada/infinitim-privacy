# InfiniTim Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 8, 2026

InfiniTim ("the app") is a puzzle game published by ATC. This policy explains what information the app handles.

## Information we collect
InfiniTim does not require an account, and we do not ask for your name, email address, or contact details inside the app.

- **Game progress** (stars earned, unlocks, settings) is stored on your device. If you sign in to Google Play Games, your progress, achievements and leaderboard scores are also stored by Google Play Games under your Google account, subject to Google's privacy policy.
- **Advertising.** The app shows ads through Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and use the advertising ID, IP address, device and app information, and ad interaction data to serve and measure ads and prevent fraud. Where required, the app asks for your consent before showing personalised ads, and you can change your choice later in Settings. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings.
- **Purchases.** If you buy "Remove ads", the purchase is processed by Google Play. We receive confirmation that the purchase succeeded, but not your payment details.

## How we use information
Information is used only to run the game, save your progress, show and measure ads, and process purchases. We do not sell your personal information.

## Sharing
Data is shared only with the service providers described above (Google AdMob, Google Play Games, Google Play Billing), which handle it under their own privacy policies.

## Children
InfiniTim is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

## Your choices
You can change ad consent in the app's Settings, reset your advertising ID in Android settings, sign out of Play Games, or uninstall the app to remove locally stored data. To ask about data held by us, contact us below.

## Changes
We may update this policy and will change the date above when we do.
